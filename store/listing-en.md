## Full description (4000 max)

invest-pal is a place to learn how markets work without risking anything.

Every account starts with virtual money. Nothing you do here touches real
funds, and nothing here is investment advice — it is a teaching tool, and it
says so on every screen.

**Trade the way a real broker works**

Market, limit, stop, scheduled and trailing-stop orders. Real spreads and
commissions, with a choice of cost profiles modelled on how different brokers
actually charge. You cannot sell what you do not hold unless you deliberately
switch short selling on, and you cannot spend money you do not have — the same
refusals a real account gives you, with the reason stated plainly.

**Buy by amount, not just by share count**

Nobody decides to buy 0.1487 of a share; they decide to put fifty dollars into
something. Enter an amount and the app does the division, and tells you when
fees are eating a large share of a small order.

**W. D. Gann's geometry, and what it means**

Angles, the Square of Nine and time cycles, computed from real price history
and drawn on the chart. An AI mentor turns each reading into two sentences of
plain language, in English or Hebrew.

A scanner ranks every measured asset and explains the top one: which side of
the 1x1 it sits on, which level it leans against, and what date the cycles
point at. It is a ranking, not advice — it describes where price has turned
before, not where it will turn.

**Built to be read**

Full Hebrew and English, right-to-left throughout. A one-tap text size control.
Designed and tested on phones down to 320 pixels wide.

**What this is not**

Not a broker. Not real money. Not investment advice. Not a prediction.
